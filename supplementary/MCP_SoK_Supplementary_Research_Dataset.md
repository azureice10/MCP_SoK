## 1. Purpose and Structure of This Supplementary Dataset
In accordance with the journal’s editorial policy limiting non-academic references in the main manuscript bibliography to no more than three essential items, this standalone **Supplementary Research Dataset** compiles all underlying primary technical sources, historical Model Context Protocol (MCP) specification revisions, individual National Vulnerability Database (NVD) Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE) records, practitioner incident disclosures, and the complete frozen 179-study synthesis corpus (including all 106 Tier E2 preprints and 88 coded defense records). Every record in this dataset is assigned a persistent identifier (`S-Spec-01`–`S-Spec-07`, `S-E4-01`–`S-E4-24`, `S-Preprint-01`–`S-Preprint-18`, `S-Corpus-001`–`S-Corpus-182` [179 active records; `072`, `085`, and `088` retired], `S-SetB-01`–`S-SetB-11`, and `S-Def-01`–`S-Def-89` [88 active records; `078` retired]) cross-referenced directly in the main manuscript text and tables.
